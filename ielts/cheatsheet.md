# IELTS Cheatsheet
Skim this before every practice session and on test day. Tools: [interactive/](interactive/index.html).

## Score targets (approx.)
| Band | Listening /40 | Academic Reading /40 |
|---|---|---|
| 6.5 | 26–29 | 27–29 |
| **7.0** | **30–31** | **30–32** |
| **7.5** | **32–34** | **33–34** |
| 8.0 | 35–36 | 35–36 |
Cut-offs vary slightly by test version (official averages: band 7 ≈ 30/40 in both). Overall = the average of 4, rounded to the nearest 0.5 (x.25 ↑ to x.5, x.75 ↑ to the next whole band). Check your universities' **per-skill minimum** too.

## Timing
Listening ~30 min (+10 min transfer on paper only) · Reading 60 min (no transfer time: ~17/20/23 min) · Writing 20 min T1 + 40 min T2 (T2 carries more weight) · Speaking 11–14 min

## Listening & Reading
- Predict the answer type · watch for corrections ("…no, actually…") · respect the word limits · spelling counts
- **F** = contradicts · **NG** = not enough information to decide · qualifiers: some ≠ most ≠ all
- Word limits: hyphenated word = 1 word, numbers as digits, plurals must match · no penalty for guessing
- Matching headings first, matching information last

## Writing Task 1 frame
1. *The [chart] illustrates/compares [what] in [where] between [when].*
2. *Overall, [main trend 1], while [main trend 2]. [Biggest/smallest feature].*  ← no numbers; band 7 needs a **clear** overview
3. Body 1: group A + data + comparison · 4. Body 2: group B + data + comparison
Trend verbs: rise, climb, surge, decline, dip, plummet, fluctuate, plateau, peak at · adverbs: sharply, steadily, marginally

## Writing Task 2 frame
1. Intro: paraphrase + **thesis** (*"I strongly agree that… because…"*)
2. Body: **topic sentence → explain → example → link back** (×2)
3. Conclusion: *"In conclusion, …"* restate the position + the main reasons
Plan 5 min · write 30 min · check 5 min (articles, plurals, agreement, tense)

Percent vs percentage point: 20% → 30% = +10 points = a 50% increase · rise (no object) vs raise (+ object)

## Linking (vary them!)
Addition: *Furthermore, Moreover, In addition* · Contrast: *However, Nevertheless, Whereas, Although* · Cause/effect: *Consequently, As a result, This leads to* · Example: *For instance, A case in point is* · Concession: *Admittedly, … Even so, …*

## Speaking
- Part 1: **answer + reason + example** (2–3 sentences)
- Part 2: scene → story → feelings → reflection. Talk the full 2 minutes.
- Part 3: **opinion → reason → example → other side/future**
- Buying time: *"That's an interesting question…"*, *"I've never really thought about it, but…"*
- Hedging: *"I'd say…"*, *"It depends on…"*, *"To some extent…"*

## Accuracy checklist
articles · subject–verb agreement (*the number of … has*) · uncountables (information, advice, research) · fewer/less · comma splices (*, however* ✗) · prepositions · consistent tense · word form · ≥150 / ≥250 words
