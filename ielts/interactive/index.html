IELTS · Interactive

Practice tools that go with the modules in ielts/. Target: Academic band 7.0–7.5.

Module 01 · Scoring
Band Calculator
Raw scores → bands, a rounding visualizer and drill, per-skill minimums, and what you need to hit your target.
 Module 02 · Listening
Listening Drills
Dictation of numbers and spellings, distractor detection, and word-limit judging. Uses your browser's voice, works offline.
 Module 03 · Reading
Reading Lab
True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given and matching headings, with the evidence highlighted in the passage.
 Module 04 · Task 1
Task 1 Trend Lab
Drag a line chart and see the right trend verb, adverb and fraction phrase. Then spot which sentences make a valid overview.
 Modules 04–05 · Writing
Writing Practice
Random Task 1 charts and Task 2 prompts, a timer, a word count, and a band-7 checklist.
 Module 06 · Speaking
Speaking Practice
Random Part 1/2/3 questions, a prep + talk timer with beeps, and recording yourself.
 Module 07 · Vocabulary
Flashcards
~40 collocation, linking, paraphrase, and grammar cards with spaced repetition.
