Reference: the five essay types
TypePrompt signalYour job
OpinionTo what extent do you agree or disagree?One clear position, the same from intro to conclusion. Partial agreement is fine if it's clear.
DiscussionDiscuss both views and give your own opinion.Explain both views fairly + state your view.
Advantages / disadvantagesDo the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?Both sides + a clear verdict on which outweighs.
Problem (cause) / solutionWhat problems / causes… What solutions…?Answer both questions.
Two-part (direct questions)Two different questions, e.g. Why…? Is this positive or negative?Answer each question explicitly.
