# IELTS Resources
⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

## Official (use these first; they're the most accurate)
- ⭐ **[IELTS official site (ielts.org)](https://ielts.org/)**: test format, [sample test questions](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test), [how scoring works](https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/ielts-scoring-in-detail), and the **public band descriptors**: [Writing](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf) (updated May 2023) · [Speaking](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-downloadable-assets/ielts-guidance-and-support/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf).
- ⭐ **Cambridge IELTS Academic** books (numbered volumes, each with 4 full tests, answer keys, transcripts, and examiner-marked model answers) 💲. The newest volumes (**IELTS 20**, 2025; **IELTS 21**, 2026) are closest to the current test.
- **[British Council: Take IELTS](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/)**: free practice tests and the **IELTS Ready** preparation course. (The British Council now offers IELTS on computer only.)
- **[IDP IELTS](https://ielts.idp.com/)**: free practice materials and webinars (IDP also runs tests in many countries, some still on paper).
- **[IELTS One Skill Retake](https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/verifying-ielts-results/one-skill-retake)**: rules and which institutions accept it.

## Teachers & free lessons
- ⭐ **[IELTS Liz](https://ieltsliz.com/)**: free lessons for every question type, model answers, and topic lists.
- **[IELTS Advantage (Chris Pell)](https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/)**: writing structures and band-7+ strategy.
- **E2 IELTS** (YouTube): test walkthroughs and speaking mocks.

## Language building
- **English Collocations in Use: Advanced** 💲 · **Advanced Grammar in Use** 💲 · **[Academic Word List](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/academicwordlist)**.
- **[Oxford Learner's Dictionaries](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/)** · **[Ozdic collocations](https://ozdic.com/)**.

## Daily input (Reading & Listening)
- Read: *The Economist*, *The Guardian*, *Scientific American*, *New Scientist*, *The Conversation*.
- Listen: BBC Learning English (*6 Minute English*), BBC *In Our Time*, TED talks, university lecture podcasts.

## Practice partners & feedback
- A teacher or a study partner for Writing feedback and Speaking mocks. This matters more than any book.
- **[This repo's interactive tools](interactive/index.html)**: band calculator, listening drills, reading lab, Task 1 trend lab, essay builder, writing timer, speaking practice, answer upgrader, grammar error hunter, flashcards.
