# IELTS Resources
⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

## Official (use these first; they're the most accurate)
- ⭐ **[IELTS official site (ielts.org)](https://ielts.org/)**: test format, free sample tests, and the **public band descriptors** for Writing and Speaking.
- ⭐ **Cambridge IELTS Academic** books (numbered volumes, each with 4 full tests, answer keys, transcripts, and examiner-marked model answers) 💲. The newest volumes are closest to the current test.
- **[British Council: Take IELTS](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/)**: free practice tests and the **IELTS Ready** preparation course.
- **IDP IELTS**: free practice materials and webinars (IDP also runs tests in many countries).

## Teachers & free lessons
- ⭐ **[IELTS Liz](https://ieltsliz.com/)**: free lessons for every question type, model answers, and topic lists.
- **[IELTS Advantage (Chris Pell)](https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/)**: writing structures and band-7+ strategy.
- **[E2 IELTS (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@E2IELTS)**: test walkthroughs and speaking mocks.

## Language building
- **English Collocations in Use: Advanced** 💲 · **Advanced Grammar in Use** 💲 · **[Academic Word List](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/academicwordlist)**.
- **[Oxford Learner's Dictionaries](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/)** · **[Ozdic collocations](https://ozdic.com/)**.

## Daily input (Reading & Listening)
- Read: *The Economist*, *The Guardian*, *Scientific American*, *New Scientist*, *The Conversation*.
- Listen: BBC Learning English (*6 Minute English*), BBC *In Our Time*, TED talks, university lecture podcasts.

## Practice partners & feedback
- A teacher or a study partner for Writing feedback and Speaking mocks. This matters more than any book.
- **[This repo's interactive tools](interactive/index.html)**: band calculator, writing timer, speaking practice, flashcards.
