# 07 · Vocabulary & Grammar for Band 7
> **Goal:** the language range band 7 requires, used accurately. · **Time:** 10–15 min/day, ongoing

👉 Drill: [IELTS flashcards](interactive/flashcards.html)

## Vocabulary: learn collocations, not lists
- Band 7's Lexical Resource rewards **collocation** and **precision**, not rare words. *"Make a decision"* (✓) vs *"do a decision"* (✗).
- **Academic Word List (AWL):** ~570 word families that appear across academic texts (analyze, significant, factor, benefit, policy…). Learn them in **phrases**.
- **Topic banks:** for each common Task 2 / Part 3 topic, 15–20 collocations, e.g.
  - *Environment:* curb emissions · renewable energy sources · carbon footprint · tackle climate change · sustainable development
  - *Education:* rote learning · critical thinking skills · tertiary education · a well-rounded curriculum
  - *Technology:* a double-edged sword · automation of jobs · digital literacy · data privacy concerns
- **Paraphrasing skill:** synonyms + changing the word form (*grow → growth*, *economic → economy*). It's essential in all four skills.
- **Method:** keep a vocabulary log (in `progress.md` or the flashcards). Each entry is a **phrase + example sentence**. Review it with spaced repetition.

## Grammar: range + accuracy
| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| Complex sentences (subordination) | *Although* remote work offers flexibility, it can blur the line between work and home life. |
| Relative clauses | Students *who* study abroad often become more independent. |
| Conditionals (1st, 2nd, 3rd, mixed) | *If* governments *invested* more in public transport, congestion *would* fall. |
| Passive voice | The data *were collected* over a decade. (Task 1 processes) |
| Nominalization | *The rapid expansion of* cities has led to… (instead of "cities expanded fast") |
| Cleft sentences | *What* concerns many experts *is* the pace of change. |
| Modal verbs for hedging | This *may/might/could* lead to… · It *is likely that*… |

**Accuracy checklist** (the most common errors at band 6–6.5): articles (*a/the/∅*), subject–verb agreement, countable/uncountable (*information*, *research*, *advice* have no plural), comma splices, tense consistency, prepositions (*interested in*, *responsible for*).
