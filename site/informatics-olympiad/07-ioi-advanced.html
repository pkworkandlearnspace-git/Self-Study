07 · IOI Skills & Advanced Topics

💻 Informatics Olympiad · Module 07
goal convert knowledge into IOI points, and learn the advanced techniques that separate medal colors.time ongoing

Contest strategy (the highest-leverage skill)#

  • First 20–30 min: read all three problems. Note every subtask and its points.
  • Bank points: implement the easy subtasks of every problem before going deep. 3 × 40 points beats 1 × 100.
  • Subtasks are hints: "n ≤ 1000", "the tree is a line", "all values distinct" each show a step toward the full solution.
  • Think before coding. Prove (or at least stress-test) your idea. With live feedback at IOI, use it to verify.
  • Keep the brute force for stress testing. Don't delete old working submissions.
  • Problem types to practice: batch (function implementation), interactive (minimize the queries), communication (two programs), output-only, constructive.

Advanced techniques (learn after 01–06)#

  • Trees: heavy-light decomposition (HLD), centroid decomposition, small-to-large merging (DSU on tree), virtual trees.
  • Data structures: persistent segment tree, segment tree beats (rare), Li Chao tree, merge-sort tree, parallel binary search, offline divide & conquer (CDQ).
  • Sqrt techniques: sqrt decomposition on queries, Mo's algorithm (with updates), heavy/light threshold splitting.
  • Graphs: max flow / min cut / bipartite matching (rarer at IOI but useful), bridges/2-edge-connected components, Euler tours.
  • DP: CHT, D&C optimization, the Aliens trick, DP over subsets / profiles.

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Past IOI problems on oj.uz (judged with subtasks) plus past national/regional olympiads (APIO, BOI, CEOI, JOI). The best practice material.
  • ⭐ USACO Guide: Platinum & Advanced.
  • IOI official site: the syllabus, past tasks, and solutions.
  • Codeforces (Div 1/2) and AtCoder (ARC/AGC) for contest practice.

Build#

  • Simulate full IOI days: 3 past-IOI problems, 5 h, scored by subtasks on oj.uz. Log the scores.
