04 · Writing Task 1 (Academic)

🗣️ IELTS Academic · Module 04
goal a reliable band 7 report in 20 minutes.time 1–2 weeks

👉 Practice with the timer: Writing practice tool

The task#

Describe visual information (line/bar/pie chart, table, process, map, or a mix) in ≥ 150 words in ~20 min. Report what's there. No opinions, no causes you invent.

The 4-paragraph structure#

  1. Introduction (1 sentence): paraphrase the prompt. "The line graph illustrates how … changed between 2000 and 2020."
  2. Overview (2 sentences): the most important paragraph. The 2–3 biggest trends or features, with no numbers. "Overall, X rose steadily, whereas Y declined. X was the highest throughout." Without a clear overview, you're capped at around band 5–6 on Task Achievement.
  3. Body 1: details + data for the first group of features.
  4. Body 2: details + data for the second group, with comparisons.

Language toolkit#

  • Trends: rise / increase / grow / climb · fall / decrease / decline / drop · fluctuate · remain stable / level off · peak at · hit a low of.
  • Degree: dramatically, sharply, significantly · steadily, gradually · slightly, marginally.
  • Two patterns: "X rose sharply" (verb + adverb) ↔ "There was a sharp rise in X" (adjective + noun). Alternate them.
  • Comparison: twice as high as, considerably more than, the largest share, by contrast, whereas, while.
  • Approximation: just under a third, roughly half, approximately 40%.
  • Processes: the passive voice + sequencers. "First, the beans are harvested. After that, they are dried…"
  • Maps: was demolished / replaced by / converted into / extended to the north.

Traps#

  • Listing every number with no grouping or comparison.
  • Missing the overview, or putting numbers in it.
  • Copying phrases from the prompt (paraphrase!). Writing < 150 words (penalized).
