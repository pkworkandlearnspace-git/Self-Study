02 · Listening

🗣️ IELTS Academic · Module 02
goal ≥ 30/40 consistently (band 7), 32+ for 7.5.time 1–2 weeks, then maintenance

The 4 parts#

  1. A conversation, everyday social context (form/note completion).
  2. A monologue, everyday context (maps, MCQ, matching).
  3. A conversation, academic context (2–4 speakers discussing an assignment).
  4. A monologue, an academic lecture (note completion, the hardest).

The audio plays once. Difficulty rises from Part 1 to Part 4.

Core techniques#

  • Predict before listening: use the reading time to underline keywords, and predict the type of answer (number? noun? plural?) and grammar.
  • Follow the signposts: "however", "actually", "but then", "the main reason", "finally". Answers often follow a correction.
  • Distractors: speakers often say a wrong answer first and then correct it ("Tuesday… no, sorry, Thursday"). Wait for the final answer.
  • Paraphrase: the question rarely uses the same words as the audio. Train your ear for synonyms.
  • Spelling and word limits count: "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" means a 3-word answer is wrong. Practice spelling numbers, dates, and common words (accommodation, environment, necessary…).
  • Don't get stuck: if you miss one, move on immediately or you lose the next three.
  • Maps: orient yourself first (entrance, "north", "opposite", "next to", "on your left").

Practice routine#

  1. Do one section under test conditions.
  2. Mark it. For every error, listen again with the transcript and label the cause: spelling / distractor / missed paraphrase / lost place / vocabulary.
  3. Log the causes in progress.md. Fix the most frequent one first.
  4. Daily ear training: 15 min of podcasts/lectures at natural speed (BBC 6 Minute English → TED → university lectures).

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Cambridge IELTS Academic books (official past-style tests) 💲, the newest volumes first.
  • IELTS official free practice tests.
  • British Council: IELTS preparation (free practice and the IELTS Ready course).
