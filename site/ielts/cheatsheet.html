IELTS Cheatsheet

Skim this before every practice session and on test day.

Score targets (approx.)#

Band Listening /40 Academic Reading /40
6.5 26–29 27–29
7.0 30–31 30–32
7.5 32–34 33–34
8.0 35–36 35–36
Overall = the average of 4, rounded to the nearest 0.5 (x.25 ↑ to x.5, x.75 ↑ to the next whole band).

Timing#

Listening ~30 min · Reading 60 min (no transfer time: ~17/20/23 min) · Writing 20 min T1 + 40 min T2 · Speaking 11–14 min

Listening & Reading#

  • Predict the answer type · watch for corrections ("…no, actually…") · respect the word limits · spelling counts
  • F = contradicts · NG = not mentioned · do matching headings first, matching information last

Writing Task 1 frame#

  1. The [chart] illustrates/compares [what] in [where] between [when].
  2. Overall, [main trend 1], while [main trend 2]. [Biggest/smallest feature]. ← no numbers
  3. Body 1: group A + data + comparison · 4. Body 2: group B + data + comparison Trend verbs: rise, climb, surge, decline, dip, plummet, fluctuate, plateau, peak at · adverbs: sharply, steadily, marginally

Writing Task 2 frame#

  1. Intro: paraphrase + thesis ("I strongly agree that… because…")
  2. Body: topic sentence → explain → example → link back (×2)
  3. Conclusion: "In conclusion, …" restate the position + the main reasons Plan 5 min · write 30 min · check 5 min (articles, plurals, agreement, tense)

Linking (vary them!)#

Addition: Furthermore, Moreover, In addition · Contrast: However, Nevertheless, Whereas, Although · Cause/effect: Consequently, As a result, This leads to · Example: For instance, A case in point is · Concession: Admittedly, … Even so, …

Speaking#

  • Part 1: answer + reason + example (2–3 sentences)
  • Part 2: scene → story → feelings → reflection. Talk the full 2 minutes.
  • Part 3: opinion → reason → example → other side/future
  • Buying time: "That's an interesting question…", "I've never really thought about it, but…"
  • Hedging: "I'd say…", "It depends on…", "To some extent…"

Accuracy checklist#

articles · subject–verb agreement · uncountables (information, advice, research) · comma splices · prepositions · consistent tense · ≥150 / ≥250 words
