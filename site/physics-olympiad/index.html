Full roadmap

Starting point: you've passed school/regional rounds and are strong at math and physics. Target: national team selection, then IPhO-level theory and experiment.

⚠️ Read first: eligibility and format#

  • IPhO contestants generally must be under 20 and not yet university students. As a senior, this cycle is likely your last. Check your national rules and selection dates now.
  • IPhO format: a theory exam (3 long multi-part problems, 5 h, 30 points) and an experimental exam (1–2 problems, 5 h, 20 points). 40% of the score is experimental, and many self-learners neglect it.
  • Problems are long and guided. Each problem builds a model step by step, so careful approximation and modeling beat obscure formulas.

The map#

# Module Time You can…
01 Math methods & estimation 1–2 wk approximations, dimensional analysis, ODEs, vector calculus basics
02 Mechanics 5–7 wk rigid bodies, oscillations, orbits, non-inertial frames, fluids
03 Thermodynamics & statistical physics 3–4 wk heat engines, kinetic theory, Boltzmann, radiation
04 Electromagnetism 5–7 wk fields, circuits, induction, image charges, EM waves
05 Waves & optics 2–3 wk interference, diffraction, geometric optics, Doppler
06 Modern physics 3–4 wk special relativity, quantum basics, atomic and nuclear physics
07 Experimental physics ongoing uncertainties, linearization, graphing, designing measurements

Order: 01 → 02 first (mechanics underlies everything). Then rotate 03–06. Run 07 every week from the start.

Interactive tools → interactive/#

  • Orbit simulator: set the launch speed and see circles, ellipses, and escape. Kepler's laws and energy live.
  • Driven damped oscillator: resonance curve, phase lag, Q-factor, transients.
  • Wave interference: two-source pattern + double-slit/grating intensity.
  • Linear fit & uncertainty: least squares with slope/intercept errors, for the experimental exam.
  • Flashcards: formulas, constants, and standard results.

Weekly routine (~10–12 h)#

Block Do
3 × 2 h Theory: Kalda/Zhou handout section → 3–5 problems
1 × 2 h Experiment: a home experiment or a past IPhO/APhO experimental problem's data analysis
1 × 3 h Mock (from week 4): one full past theory problem in 1h40, then grade strictly with the official marking scheme
30 min Update progress.md and review flashcards
