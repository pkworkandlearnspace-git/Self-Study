07 · Experimental Physics

⚛️ Physics Olympiad · Module 07
goal earn the 20 experimental points. It's the most trainable part of the IPhO.time ~2 h/week, ongoing

👉 Interactive: Linear fit & uncertainty tool

Skip test#

Core ideas#

  • Uncertainty: report x=xˉ±Δxx = \bar x \pm \Delta xx=xˉ±Δx with 1–2 significant figures in Δx\Delta xΔx, and the value rounded to match.
    • Sum/difference: absolute errors add in quadrature: Δ(a±b)=Δa2+Δb2\Delta(a\pm b) = \sqrt{\Delta a^2+\Delta b^2}Δ(a±b)=Δa2+Δb2​.
    • Product/power: relative errors add in quadrature: Δff=(nΔaa)2+(mΔbb)2\frac{\Delta f}{f} = \sqrt{\left(n\frac{\Delta a}{a}\right)^2 + \left(m\frac{\Delta b}{b}\right)^2}fΔf​=(naΔa​)2+(mbΔb​)2​ for f=anbmf = a^nb^mf=anbm.
    • Repeated measurements: standard error of the mean σ/N\sigma/\sqrt Nσ/N​.
  • Linearization: transform the data so the theory predicts a straight line (T2T^2T2 vs LLL; ln⁡I\ln IlnI vs xxx; ln⁡y\ln ylny vs ln⁡x\ln xlnx). The slope/intercept then give the physical constants.
  • Graphing at IPhO (graded!): a title, labeled axes with units, sensible scales (the data fills ≥ ½ the page), error bars, a best-fit line, and the slope read off a large triangle.
  • Experimental design: measure many periods, vary over a wide range, repeat, spot outliers, control variables, and check for systematics (zero offset, parallax, timing reaction).
  • Read the problem's instructions on rounding and on which method to use. Points are given per step.

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Past IPhO / APhO / EuPhO experimental problems with marking schemes (see resources). Practice the data-analysis parts even without the apparatus.
  • ⭐ Kalda: Experimental study guide.
  • An Introduction to Error Analysis, John Taylor 💲: the standard text (ch. 1–8).

Home experiments (cheap)#

  1. Pendulum → ggg to < 1% (linearize T2T^2T2 vs LLL).
  2. CD/DVD as a diffraction grating with a laser pointer → track spacing.
  3. Cooling coffee cup → Newton's law of cooling (fit ln⁡(T−Troom)\ln(T - T_{room})ln(T−Troom​) vs ttt).
  4. A bouncing ball → coefficient of restitution from the sound of the bounces (phone audio recorder).
  5. A smartphone sensor app (e.g. phyphox) → acceleration, rotation, magnetic field, sound frequency.
