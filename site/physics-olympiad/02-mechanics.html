02 · Mechanics

⚛️ Physics Olympiad · Module 02
goal the backbone of every IPhO (often 1–2 of the 3 theory problems).time 5–7 weeks

👉 Interactive: Orbit simulator · Driven damped oscillator

Skip test#

Core ideas#

  • Kinematics: projectiles, relative motion, constraints (strings, rods, rolling: v=ωRv = \omega Rv=ωR).
  • Newton's laws: free-body diagrams. Choose axes wisely. Non-inertial frames add −maframe-m\mathbf a_{frame}−maframe​, centrifugal mω2rm\omega^2rmω2r, and Coriolis −2mω×v-2m\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf v−2mω×v.
  • Conservation laws: energy (work–energy, potential energy), momentum (collisions, CM frame), angular momentum (central forces, a skater pulling in her arms). Ask which quantity is conserved before writing equations.
  • Rigid bodies: moment of inertia (parallel/perpendicular axis theorems), torque τ=r×F\boldsymbol\tau = \mathbf r\times\mathbf Fτ=r×F, τ=Iα\tau = I\alphaτ=Iα about the CM or a fixed axis, rolling, the instantaneous axis of rotation, statics (ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0 about any point).
  • Oscillations: SHM, small-oscillation approximations, energy method, coupled oscillators and normal modes, damped/driven oscillators, resonance, the Q-factor.
  • Gravitation: Kepler's laws, the effective potential Ueff=−GMmr+L22mr2U_{eff} = -\frac{GMm}{r}+\frac{L^2}{2mr^2}Ueff​=−rGMm​+2mr2L2​, orbital energy E=−GMm2aE = -\frac{GMm}{2a}E=−2aGMm​, the shell theorem.
  • Variable mass: rockets, chains, conveyor belts.
  • Fluids: hydrostatics, Archimedes, Bernoulli, continuity, surface tension (Laplace pressure Δp=2σ/R\Delta p = 2\sigma/RΔp=2σ/R), viscosity basics.
  • 💡 Lagrangian mechanics is optional at IPhO but often a shortcut for complicated constraints. Learn it after the Newtonian toolkit is solid.

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Jaan Kalda: Mechanics study guide: a compact list of "ideas" with olympiad problems. Built specifically for IPhO. Also see Kevin Zhou's mechanics (M) handouts.
  • ⭐ Introduction to Classical Mechanics, David Morin 💲: the best problem-based textbook. Ch. 1–9.
  • 200 Puzzling Physics Problems, Gnädig, Honyek & Riley 💲 · Problems in General Physics, Irodov (classic and hard).

Build#

  • Solve every Kalda mechanics idea's associated problems. Then do 5 past IPhO mechanics problems under time pressure.

Traps#

  • Taking torques about a point that's accelerating (only the CM or a fixed point is safe for τ=Iα\tau = I\alphaτ=Iα).
  • Forgetting friction can point either way. Assume a direction and let the sign tell you.
