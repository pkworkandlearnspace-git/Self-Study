04 · Electromagnetism

⚛️ Physics Olympiad · Module 04
goal fields, circuits, and induction at the level of IPhO's long problems.time 5–7 weeks

Skip test#

Core ideas#

  • Electrostatics: Coulomb, superposition, E=−∇V\mathbf E = -\nabla VE=−∇V. Gauss's law ∮E⋅dA=Q/ε0\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf A = Q/\varepsilon_0∮E⋅dA=Q/ε0​ (use symmetry!).
  • Conductors: E = 0 inside, the surface is an equipotential. Method of images. Capacitance, energy 12CV2\frac12CV^221​CV2, energy density 12ε0E2\frac12\varepsilon_0E^221​ε0​E2.
  • Dielectrics: εr\varepsilon_rεr​, polarization (qualitative/basic).
  • Circuits: Kirchhoff's laws, series/parallel, Thevenin equivalent, infinite ladders (self-similarity), RC/RL/LC/RLC transients, AC with complex impedance (ZL=iωLZ_L = i\omega LZL​=iωL, ZC=1/(iωC)Z_C = 1/(i\omega C)ZC​=1/(iωC)), non-linear elements (diodes, graphically).
  • Magnetostatics: Lorentz force q(E+v×B)q(\mathbf E + \mathbf v\times\mathbf B)q(E+v×B), Biot–Savart, Ampère's law, magnetic dipoles, the force on a current-carrying wire.
  • Induction: Faraday E=−dΦ/dt\mathcal E = -d\Phi/dtE=−dΦ/dt, Lenz's law, motional EMF vBLvBLvBL, self/mutual inductance, energy 12LI2\frac12LI^221​LI2, eddy currents.
  • Maxwell's equations & EM waves: displacement current, c=1/μ0ε0c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}c=1/μ0​ε0​​, the Poynting vector, radiation pressure.
  • 🔭 Analogies: thermal circuits ↔ electric circuits; gravity ↔ electrostatics (Gauss).

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Kalda: Electricity & Magnetism study guide + Kevin Zhou's E&M (E) handouts.
  • ⭐ Electricity and Magnetism, Purcell & Morin (3rd ed.) 💲: the olympiad-standard E&M textbook.
  • Introduction to Electrodynamics, Griffiths 💲: ch. 1–7, for more mathematical depth.

Build#

  • Solve 5 infinite/symmetric circuit puzzles. Derive the field of a uniformly magnetized sphere (stretch). Do 3 past IPhO E&M problems.
