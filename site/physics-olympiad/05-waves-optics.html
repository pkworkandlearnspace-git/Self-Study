05 · Waves & Optics

⚛️ Physics Olympiad · Module 05
goal interference, diffraction, and geometric optics. These show up in both the theory and the experiment.time 2–3 weeks

👉 Interactive: Wave interference & diffraction

Skip test#

Core ideas#

  • Waves: y=Asin⁡(kx−ωt)y = A\sin(kx - \omega t)y=Asin(kx−ωt), v=ω/k=fλv = \omega/k = f\lambdav=ω/k=fλ. The wave equation. String speed v=T/μv = \sqrt{T/\mu}v=T/μ​, sound speed. Standing waves and harmonics. Beats.
  • Superposition: phase difference Δϕ=2πλΔ(path)\Delta\phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\Delta(\text{path})Δϕ=λ2π​Δ(path) + reflection phase. Constructive at Δϕ=2πm\Delta\phi = 2\pi mΔϕ=2πm.
  • Interference: double slit, thin films (π phase shift on reflection off a higher-n medium), Newton's rings, Michelson interferometer.
  • Diffraction: single slit minima at asin⁡θ=mλa\sin\theta = m\lambdaasinθ=mλ, diffraction grating maxima at dsin⁡θ=mλd\sin\theta = m\lambdadsinθ=mλ. Intensity = (single-slit envelope) × (multi-slit interference). Rayleigh criterion.
  • Geometric optics: Fermat's principle, Snell's law, total internal reflection, the thin lens/mirror equation 1f=1do+1di\frac1f = \frac1{d_o}+\frac1{d_i}f1​=do​1​+di​1​, magnification, optical instruments (telescope, microscope), prisms and dispersion.
  • Polarization: Malus's law I=I0cos⁡2θI = I_0\cos^2\thetaI=I0​cos2θ, Brewster's angle tan⁡θB=n2/n1\tan\theta_B = n_2/n_1tanθB​=n2​/n1​.
  • Doppler effect: sound f′=fv±vov∓vsf' = f\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}f′=fv∓vs​v±vo​​. Relativistic Doppler in module 06.
