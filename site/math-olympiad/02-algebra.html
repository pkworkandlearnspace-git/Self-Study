02 · Algebra

🧮 Math Olympiad · Module 02
goal inequalities, polynomials, functional equations, and sequences at national-to-IMO level.time 4–6 weeks (in rotation)

Skip test#

Core ideas#

Inequalities#

  • AM-GM: x1+⋯+xnn≥x1⋯xnn\frac{x_1+\dots+x_n}{n}\ge\sqrt[n]{x_1\cdots x_n}nx1​+⋯+xn​​≥nx1​⋯xn​​, with equality iff all are equal. Weighted version too.
  • Power mean chain: QM ≥ AM ≥ GM ≥ HM.
  • Cauchy–Schwarz: (∑ai2)(∑bi2)≥(∑aibi)2\left(\sum a_i^2\right)\left(\sum b_i^2\right)\ge\left(\sum a_ib_i\right)^2(∑ai2​)(∑bi2​)≥(∑ai​bi​)2. Titu/Engel form: ∑ai2bi≥(∑ai)2∑bi\sum\frac{a_i^2}{b_i}\ge\frac{(\sum a_i)^2}{\sum b_i}∑bi​ai2​​≥∑bi​(∑ai​)2​.
  • Hölder, rearrangement, Chebyshev, Schur (t>0t>0t>0: ∑at(a−b)(a−c)≥0\sum a^t(a-b)(a-c)\ge0∑at(a−b)(a−c)≥0), Muirhead (for symmetric polynomial inequalities).
  • Jensen: for convex fff, f ⁣(∑xin)≤∑f(xi)nf\!\left(\frac{\sum x_i}{n}\right)\le\frac{\sum f(x_i)}{n}f(n∑xi​​)≤n∑f(xi​)​. Tangent line trick for ∑f(xi)\sum f(x_i)∑f(xi​) with ∑xi\sum x_i∑xi​ fixed.
  • Techniques: homogenize, normalize (a+b+c=1a+b+c=1a+b+c=1 or abc=1abc=1abc=1), substitution (a=xya=\frac{x}{y}a=yx​, Ravi a=y+za=y+za=y+z for triangle sides), SOS (sum of squares), and uvwuvwuvw / mixing variables.
  • Always find the equality case first. It tells you which inequality can work.

Polynomials#

  • Factor theorem, Vieta's formulas, Rational root theorem.
  • Integer-coefficient trick: a−b∣P(a)−P(b)a-b \mid P(a)-P(b)a−b∣P(a)−P(b).
  • Roots of unity filter: 1n∑k=0n−1P(ωk)\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}P(\omega^k)n1​∑k=0n−1​P(ωk) extracts the coefficients with index ≡ 0 (mod n).
  • Lagrange interpolation, finite differences (a degree-ddd polynomial has a constant ddd-th difference).
  • Irreducibility: Eisenstein's criterion; the mod-ppp reduction trick.

Functional equations#

  • Standard plays: substitute x=y=0x=y=0x=y=0, y=0y=0y=0, y=xy=xy=x, y=−xy=-xy=−x, x=1x=1x=1. Find f(0)f(0)f(0) and f(1)f(1)f(1).
  • Prove injective / surjective, find fixed points, use symmetry (swap x,yx,yx,y).
  • Cauchy's equation f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y): over Q\mathbb QQ gives f(x)=cxf(x)=cxf(x)=cx. Over R\mathbb RR it needs extra conditions (monotonic, bounded on an interval, or continuous).
  • Guess the answer (usually f(x)=xf(x)=xf(x)=x, cxcxcx, x2x^2x2, constant), verify it, then prove it's the only one.
  • Over N\mathbb NN / Z\mathbb ZZ: induction, bounding, and number theory.

Sequences & sums#

  • Linear recurrences: characteristic equation. Telescoping. Bounding by monotonicity/convergence.

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Evan Chen: "A Brief Introduction to Olympiad Inequalities", plus his functional equations material on the handouts page.
  • ⭐ Yufei Zhao's olympiad handouts: inequalities, polynomials, and more.
  • Secrets in Inequalities, Pham Kim Hung 💲 · Functional Equations, Venkatachala 💲 · Problems from the Book, Andreescu & Dospinescu 💲 (advanced).
  • AoPS: Intermediate Algebra 💲 if you need a gentler ramp.

Build#

  • Solve the A1–A4 problems from the last 5 years of IMO Shortlists. Make a one-line "key idea" note for each.

Traps#

  • Applying AM-GM where the equality case can't happen.
  • In functional equations, forgetting to check the solution in the original equation, and forgetting the pathological solutions of Cauchy.
