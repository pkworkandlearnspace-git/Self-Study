Math Olympiad Resources

⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

Books by stage#

Stage Book Use for
Ramp ⭐ The Art and Craft of Problem Solving, Zeitz 💲 strategies, first proofs
Ramp AoPS Volume 1 & 2, Intermediate Algebra, Intermediate Counting, Intro to NT 💲 filling gaps
National ⭐ Problem-Solving Strategies, Engel 💲 huge problem bank by technique
National ⭐ EGMO, Evan Chen 💲 geometry
National Modern Olympiad Number Theory, Khurmi (free PDF, on AoPS) number theory
National Problem Solving Methods in Combinatorics, Soberón 💲 combinatorics
IMO Problems from the Book, Andreescu & Dospinescu 💲 hard algebra/NT
IMO Lemmas in Olympiad Geometry, Andreescu et al. 💲 geometry lemmas
IMO Secrets in Inequalities, Pham Kim Hung 💲 inequalities

Free handouts (the best free material)#

  • ⭐ Evan Chen's handouts: inequalities, orders, functional equations, geometry, proof writing.
  • ⭐ Yufei Zhao's olympiad handouts: from MOP (the US training camp).
  • Evan Chen's Napkin: higher math for olympiad students (optional, beyond the IMO).

Problems & community#

  • ⭐ AoPS Contest Collections: past olympiads with solutions.
  • ⭐ IMO official site: problems, rules, and results.
  • AoPS forums: ask questions, read solution discussions.
  • MOHS hardness scale (Evan Chen): difficulty ratings so you can pick problems at the right level.

Tools#

  • GeoGebra: accurate geometry figures.
  • This repo's interactive tools: triangle centers, modular lab, flashcards.
