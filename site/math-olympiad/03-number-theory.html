03 · Number Theory

🧮 Math Olympiad · Module 03
goal divisibility, modular arithmetic, orders, and Diophantine equations at IMO level.time 4–6 weeks (in rotation)

👉 Use the Modular arithmetic lab to see orders and primitive roots.

Skip test#

Core ideas#

  • Divisibility & gcd: Euclid's algorithm, Bézout (ax+by=gcd⁡(a,b)ax+by=\gcd(a,b)ax+by=gcd(a,b)), gcd⁡(am−1,an−1)=agcd⁡(m,n)−1\gcd(a^m-1,a^n-1)=a^{\gcd(m,n)}-1gcd(am−1,an−1)=agcd(m,n)−1.
  • Fundamental theorem of arithmetic. ppp-adic valuation vp(n)v_p(n)vp​(n): think of numbers as exponent vectors.
  • Modular arithmetic: inverses exist iff gcd⁡(a,n)=1\gcd(a,n)=1gcd(a,n)=1. CRT: you can solve congruences modulo coprime moduli independently.
  • Fermat: ap−1≡1(modp)a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod pap−1≡1(modp). Euler: aφ(n)≡1(modn)a^{\varphi(n)}\equiv1\pmod naφ(n)≡1(modn). Wilson: (p−1)!≡−1(modp)(p-1)!\equiv-1\pmod p(p−1)!≡−1(modp).
  • Order: ordn(a)\text{ord}_n(a)ordn​(a) = the smallest ddd with ad≡1a^d\equiv1ad≡1. Key lemma: ak≡1  ⟺  ord∣ka^k\equiv1 \iff \text{ord}\mid kak≡1⟺ord∣k. This is the most-used idea in olympiad NT.
  • Primitive roots exist mod 1,2,4,pk,2pk1, 2, 4, p^k, 2p^k1,2,4,pk,2pk. So (Z/p)×(\mathbb Z/p)^\times(Z/p)× is cyclic.
  • Quadratic residues: Legendre symbol, Euler's criterion (ap)≡ap−12\left(\frac ap\right)\equiv a^{\frac{p-1}2}(pa​)≡a2p−1​, −1-1−1 is a QR mod ppp iff p≡1(mod4)p\equiv1\pmod4p≡1(mod4). Quadratic reciprocity.
  • Lifting The Exponent (LTE): for odd prime p∣a−bp\mid a-bp∣a−b, p∤a,bp\nmid a,bp∤a,b: vp(an−bn)=vp(a−b)+vp(n)v_p(a^n-b^n)=v_p(a-b)+v_p(n)vp​(an−bn)=vp​(a−b)+vp​(n). (Special rules for p=2p=2p=2.)
  • Legendre's formula: vp(n!)=∑k≥1⌊n/pk⌋v_p(n!)=\sum_{k\ge1}\lfloor n/p^k\rfloorvp​(n!)=∑k≥1​⌊n/pk⌋.
  • Zsigmondy's theorem (use with care; graders may want a proof): an−bna^n-b^nan−bn has a primitive prime divisor, apart from listed exceptions.
  • Diophantine equations: factor, work mod mmm to rule out cases, bound sizes (inequalities!), use infinite descent, Pell equations, Vieta jumping.
  • Floor functions: write x=⌊x⌋+{x}x = \lfloor x\rfloor + \{x\}x=⌊x⌋+{x}. Hermite's identity.

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Modern Olympiad Number Theory, Aditya Khurmi: a free PDF shared by the author on AoPS (search the title). Thorough and olympiad-focused.
  • Evan Chen's NT handouts (orders) · Yufei Zhao: NT handouts.
  • Number Theory: Structures, Examples, and Problems, Andreescu & Andrica 💲 · 104 Number Theory Problems, Andreescu et al. 💲.
  • AoPS: Introduction to Number Theory 💲 if you need the basics.

Build#

  • Use the lab to find the order of every aaa mod 13, then prove the pattern you see (the orders divide 12; there are exactly φ(d)\varphi(d)φ(d) elements of order ddd).
  • Solve N1–N4 from recent IMO Shortlists.

Traps#

  • Dividing in congruences without checking that the inverse exists.
  • Forgetting the p=2p=2p=2 special case of LTE and quadratic residues.
