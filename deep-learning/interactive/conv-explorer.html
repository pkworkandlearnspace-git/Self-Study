Recursion, layer by layer: r ← r + d(k−1)·j, then j ← j·s (start with r = j = 1). Strides multiply the jump, so downsampling early makes the receptive field grow fast. Two 3×3 convs see 5×5 with 18 weights per channel pair instead of 25, plus an extra nonlinearity: that is the VGG argument.
