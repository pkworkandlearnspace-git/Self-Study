Experiments

  1. 0 hidden layers = logistic regression. It can only draw a straight line, so it fails on Circle and XOR.
  2. XOR with 1 layer of 2 neurons: just barely possible. Re-initialize a few times and see how often it gets stuck.
  3. Spiral: needs depth. Compare 1×16 against 3×8.
  4. Sigmoid with 3 layers trains much slower than ReLU/tanh: vanishing gradients.
  5. High noise + a big net: the train loss keeps falling while the test loss rises. That's overfitting. Add L2 to fight it.
  6. Learning rate 1.0: the loss explodes or oscillates.
