Diffusion & Flow Lab

A 2D toy where the "trained network" is perfect: the data is a mixture of Gaussians, so the noisy score ∇log pt and the flow-matching velocity have exact formulas. That isolates the sampler: compare DDPM, DDIM and flow matching at different step counts, and see what classifier-free guidance does.

1 · Forward process: data → noise

ᾱt (signal fraction of variance)
signal scale √ᾱt
noise scale √(1−ᾱt)
SNR = ᾱ/(1−ᾱ)

Closed form, no simulation needed: xt = √ᾱt·x0 + √(1−ᾱt)·ε. Arrows point toward higher density of the noisy distribution pt. At large t they all point to the origin; at small t they point to the nearest data cluster.

2 · Reverse: noise → samples

Press Sample.
DDPM adds fresh noise each step, so it needs many steps. DDIM and flow matching follow a deterministic ODE; flow matching's paths are straighter, so few Euler steps already land close to the data.
Part of the Self-Study repo · deep-learning/interactive · Guidance uses ε̂ = ε̂uncond + w(ε̂cond − ε̂uncond): w = 0 is unconditional, w = 1 is the plain conditional model, w > 1 over-emphasizes the class.
