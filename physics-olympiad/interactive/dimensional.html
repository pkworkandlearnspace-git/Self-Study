Dimensional Analysis Solver

Give the quantity you want and the quantities it may depend on, each as powers of M, L, T, Θ (temperature) and I (current). The solver matches exponents, like you would by hand, and finds the dimensionless groups (Buckingham π). Try predicting the answer before pressing Solve (module 01).

Row 1 (shaded) is the target. Exponents can be fractions such as -0.5.

How to read the result

  • Unique answer (no dimensionless group among the inputs): the target is fixed up to a pure number C. Dimensional analysis can never give C.
  • One or more π groups: the answer is target = (power law) × f(π₁, π₂, …) with f unknown. Physics (a limit, a symmetry, linearity) has to pin f down.
  • No solution: the target cannot be built from your inputs. You have missed a variable, often g, a constant like G or kB, or a density.
