Scenarios

Lorentz transformation (c = 1 units)

  • t′ = γ(t − βx), x′ = γ(x − βt), γ = 1/√(1 − β²).
  • Interval Δs² = (cΔt)² − Δx² is the same in every frame. Δs² > 0: timelike, so every frame agrees on the order and a signal could connect them. Δs² < 0: spacelike, so some frame sees them simultaneous and the order can flip.
  • The ct′ axis is the worldline x = βct of the S′ origin. The x′ axis is the line of S′-simultaneity, ct = βx. Both lean toward the light line as β → 1.
  • Tick spacing on the S′ axes is stretched by √((1 + β²)/(1 − β²)) on paper. The hyperbolas (ct)² − x² = 1 pass through each frame's unit ticks.
