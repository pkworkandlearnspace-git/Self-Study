Formulas used

Unweighted least squares (no σ given): Δ = NΣX² − (ΣX)², slope m = (NΣXY − ΣXΣY)/Δ, intercept b = (ΣY − mΣX)/N. The scatter s² = Σ(Y − mX − b)²/(N − 2) gives σm = s√(N/Δ) and σb = s√(ΣX²/Δ).

Weighted (σy given for every point): same formulas with weights w = 1/σY², with σY = |dY/dy|·σy propagated through the Y transform. Then σm = √(Σw/Δw), σb = √(Σwx²/Δw), and χ²/dof = Σw(Y − mX − b)²/(N − 2) should be close to 1.

σx too (fourth column): the effective-variance method, σeff² = σY² + (m σX)², refitted until m stops changing.

Max/min slope lines (the "by hand" method accepted in exams): the steepest and shallowest lines through the error bars of the first and last points. ½(mmax − mmin) is a quick, usually conservative, estimate of σm. Reference: J. R. Taylor, An Introduction to Error Analysis, ch. 8.
