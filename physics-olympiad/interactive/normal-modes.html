Coupled Oscillators & Normal Modes

Wall – k – m₁ – κ – m₂ – k – wall. Start in a pure normal mode and the pattern never changes. Kick one mass and the energy sloshes back and forth (beats). Predict ω₁, ω₂ and the beat period before you press play (module 02).

The physics

  • Equations: m₁ẍ₁ = −k x₁ − κ(x₁ − x₂), m₂ẍ₂ = −k x₂ − κ(x₂ − x₁). Try x = A eiωt and set det(K − ω²M) = 0.
  • Equal masses: ω₁ = √(k/m) (in phase, middle spring never stretches) and ω₂ = √((k + 2κ)/m) (anti-phase).
  • Any motion is a sum of the two modes. A kick to one mass excites both equally, so the energy moves completely across every half beat, π/(ω₂ − ω₁).
  • Unequal masses: the modes are no longer symmetric, and the energy transfer is incomplete. Try m₂/m₁ = 2.
