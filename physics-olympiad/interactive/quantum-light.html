Practice: Earth's equilibrium temperature

The Sun (T = 5772 K, R = 6.96×10⁸ m) is 1.496×10¹¹ m away. Absorbed power: (1 − A)·L/(4πd²)·πRE². Emitted: 4πRE²σTE⁴. So TE = T☉(1 − A)1/4√(R/2d).

With A = 0: TE = 5772 × √(6.96×10⁸ / 2.992×10¹¹) ≈ 5772 × 0.04823 ≈ 278 K. With A = 0.30 it is ≈ 255 K. The real mean of about 288 K is higher because of the greenhouse effect.

Stopping voltage Vs against frequency for every metal. Each line has slope h/e. It crosses zero at the threshold f₀ = φ/h. Millikan measured h this way (1916).

Raising the intensity raises the number of electrons (the current), not their maximum energy. Below the threshold frequency, no electrons come out, however bright the light.
