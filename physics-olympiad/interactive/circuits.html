RC, RL and RLC Circuits

A series circuit with a battery V₀ switched on at t = 0 (or a pre-charged capacitor discharging). Watch the transient, then switch to AC and see the resonance curve. Sketch the curve first, then check (module 04).

Key results

  • RC: τ = RC. Charging VC = V₀(1 − e−t/τ), I = (V₀/R)e−t/τ. Half the battery's energy always ends up as heat in R, whatever R is.
  • RL: τ = L/R. I = (V₀/R)(1 − e−t/τ). The inductor resists changes in current.
  • RLC: Lq̈ + Rq̇ + q/C = V₀, the same ODE as the damped oscillator with γ = R/(2L), ω₀ = 1/√(LC). Under-damped if R < 2√(L/C).
  • AC: Z = R + i(ωL − 1/(ωC)). The current peaks at ω₀ (exactly, unlike the displacement of a mechanical oscillator) and Q = ω₀L/R = ω₀/Δω.
