## Core ideas
- **Electrostatics:** Coulomb, superposition, $\mathbf E = -\nabla V$. **Gauss's law** $\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf A = Q/\varepsilon_0$ (use symmetry!).
- **Conductors:** E = 0 inside, the surface is an equipotential. **Method of images**. Capacitance, energy $\frac12CV^2$, energy density $\frac12\varepsilon_0E^2$.
- **Dielectrics:** $\varepsilon_r$, polarization (qualitative/basic).
- **Circuits:** Kirchhoff's laws, series/parallel, **Thevenin equivalent**, infinite ladders (self-similarity), RC/RL/LC/RLC transients, AC with complex impedance ($Z_L = i\omega L$, $Z_C = 1/(i\omega C)$), non-linear elements (diodes, graphically).
- **Magnetostatics:** Lorentz force $q(\mathbf E + \mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$, Biot–Savart, **Ampère's law**, magnetic dipoles, the force on a current-carrying wire.
- **Induction:** Faraday $\mathcal E = -d\Phi/dt$, Lenz's law, motional EMF $vBL$, self/mutual inductance, energy $\frac12LI^2$, eddy currents.
- **Maxwell's equations & EM waves:** displacement current, $c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}$, the Poynting vector, radiation pressure.
- 🔭 **Analogies:** thermal circuits ↔ electric circuits; gravity ↔ electrostatics (Gauss).
