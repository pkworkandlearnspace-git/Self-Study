# Physics Olympiad Resources
⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

## The core stack
1. ⭐ **[Kevin Zhou's olympiad handouts](https://knzhou.github.io/#handouts)**: complete, free, problem-rich. The best single resource. Map to this repo: P1–P2 → 01 & 07 · M1–M8 → 02 · T1–T3 → 03 · E1–E8 → 04 · W1–W3 → 05 · R1–R3, X1–X3 → 06. Start with his Syllabus/FAQ to pick an order.
2. ⭐ **[Jaan Kalda's study guides](https://www.ioc.ee/~kalda/ipho/)**: compact "idea lists" (kinematics, mechanics, E&M, thermodynamics, wave optics, experimental) with IPhO-level problems.
3. ⭐ **Past IPhO problems with official solutions and marking schemes** (below).

## Past problems
- ⭐ **[IPhO official site](https://www.ipho-new.org/)** and the **[IPhO unofficial archive](https://www.ipho-unofficial.org/)**: past theory and experimental problems, with solutions.
- **APhO** (Asian), **EuPhO** (European), and **USAPhO** ([AAPT Physics Team past exams](https://www.aapt.org/physicsteam/)): excellent intermediate-level sets.
- **[Physics Cup (Kalda)](https://physicscup.ee/)**: very hard, creative problems, with an online competition each year.
- Your country's national olympiad archive.

## Free extras
- **[The Feynman Lectures on Physics](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/)**: deep intuition.
- **[Falstad simulations](https://www.falstad.com/)**: circuits, waves, E&M.
- **[phyphox](https://phyphox.org/)**: turns your phone into a lab (for module 07).
- **[This repo's interactive tools](interactive/index.html)**.
