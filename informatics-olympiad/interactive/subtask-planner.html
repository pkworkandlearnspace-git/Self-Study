IOI Day Planner

After reading the three tasks, estimate each subtask's minutes and your chance of getting it. The planner picks the set that maximizes expected points in the time you have, which is a 0/1 knapsack over subtasks (module 07). Then compare it with "go deep on one task".

Simplification: subtasks are treated as independent. In reality, a harder subtask's solution often also solves the easier ones, so enter the extra minutes it needs beyond the previous subtask.
