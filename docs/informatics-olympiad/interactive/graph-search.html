What to notice

  • BFS finds the fewest-edges path, but it ignores weights. Add heavy cells and watch it walk straight through them.
  • DFS isn't a shortest-path algorithm at all. Its path is usually terrible, but its stack-like exploration is what you want for components, cycles, and topological order.
  • Dijkstra expands in order of distance, so it's optimal with non-negative weights. It visits a "circle" of cost.
  • A* = Dijkstra + the heuristic h = Manhattan distance. It's still optimal (h never overestimates) but visits far fewer cells.
