06 · Contest Practice & Solution Writing#

Module 06⏱ 1 min readView Markdown source

Goal: turn knowledge into points under exam conditions. · Time: ongoing from week 3

Core ideas#

  • Mock exams: 3 problems, 4.5 h, no notes, real timer. Use past national olympiads → IMO Shortlist (ISL) → past IMOs.
  • Grade strictly on the 0–7 IMO scale. A "nearly complete" proof usually earns 0–2, not 6.
  • Time allocation: first 30 min on reading all three problems and trying small cases. Spend the most time on the problem you're most likely to fully solve.
  • Partial credit: write down proven lemmas, reductions, and the answer with a construction. Graders reward substantial progress.
  • Post-mortem every mock: for each problem, what was the key idea, and what would have led you to it faster? Log it in progress.md.
  • Difficulty ladder (roughly): school/regional → national olympiad → hard national (e.g. USAMO/Balkan/APMO style) → ISL easy (A1/C1/G1/N1) → IMO P1/P4 → ISL medium → IMO P2/P5 → P3/P6.

Where to find problems#

  • ⭐ AoPS Contest Collections: almost every olympiad ever, with community solutions.
  • ⭐ IMO official site: problems & results and the IMO Shortlists (published after the following IMO).
  • Evan Chen's practice materials / OTIS excerpts and his MOHS hardness ratings (to pick problems at the right level).
  • Past papers of your own national olympiad. Ask the committee or past participants.

Done when#

  • You consistently fully solve P1/P4-level problems in mocks and have partial progress on P2/P5.
