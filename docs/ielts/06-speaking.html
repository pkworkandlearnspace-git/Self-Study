06 · Speaking#

Module 06⏱ 2 min readView Markdown source

Goal: band 7: speak at length, naturally, with flexible vocabulary. · Time: 15 min/day, 2–3 weeks focused

👉 Practice: Speaking practice tool (random questions, timers, recording)

The 3 parts#

Part Time What happens How to answer
1 4–5 min Familiar topics (home, studies, hobbies…) 2–3 sentences: answer → reason → example/detail. Don't give one-word answers, and don't give speeches.
2 1 min prep + 1–2 min talk A cue card ("Describe a time when…") Speak the full 2 minutes. Use the prep minute to jot 4–5 keywords, not sentences.
3 4–5 min Abstract discussion linked to Part 2 Opinion → reason → example → (other side / future). Talk about society in general, not only you.

Part 2 structure (works for any cue card)#

  1. Set the scene: what/who/when/where (20 s).
  2. The story/details: what happened, following the bullet points (60 s).
  3. Feelings & reflection: why it mattered, how you feel now (30 s).
  4. Filler you can always add: comparisons ("Compared with…"), a hypothetical ("If I could do it again, I'd…").

Band 7 moves#

  • Fluency over perfection. Self-correct quickly. Use natural fillers ("Well…", "Let me think…", "That's an interesting question…") rather than silence.
  • Extend: because… / for example… / on the other hand… / I suppose it depends on…
  • Vocabulary: topic-specific words + some idioms/phrasal verbs used naturally ("I was over the moon", "it boils down to"). Paraphrase when you don't know a word.
  • Grammar range: mix the tenses (past stories, present perfect experiences, conditionals for hypotheticals, future for plans).
  • Pronunciation: clear word stress, sentence stress, and intonation. Chunk your speech. Your accent is fine; clarity is what counts.

Practice routine#

  • Daily: 3 random Part 1 questions + 1 Part 2 card (timed) + 2 Part 3 questions, out loud.
  • 2–3×/week: record a Part 2, listen back, and note 3 issues (hesitations, repeated words, grammar slips). Re-record once.
  • Weekly: a mock interview with a partner/teacher, ideally someone who'll interrupt and ask follow-ups.

Learn from#

  • ⭐ IELTS Liz: Speaking (question lists + model answers).
  • E2 IELTS and IELTS Advantage (YouTube speaking strategies + mock tests).
  • Official speaking test videos on the IELTS and British Council sites.
