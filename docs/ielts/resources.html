IELTS Resources#

Resources⏱ 1 min readView Markdown source

⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

Official (use these first; they're the most accurate)#

  • ⭐ IELTS official site (ielts.org): test format, free sample tests, and the public band descriptors for Writing and Speaking.
  • ⭐ Cambridge IELTS Academic books (numbered volumes, each with 4 full tests, answer keys, transcripts, and examiner-marked model answers) 💲. The newest volumes are closest to the current test.
  • British Council: Take IELTS: free practice tests and the IELTS Ready preparation course.
  • IDP IELTS: free practice materials and webinars (IDP also runs tests in many countries).

Teachers & free lessons#

  • ⭐ IELTS Liz: free lessons for every question type, model answers, and topic lists.
  • IELTS Advantage (Chris Pell): writing structures and band-7+ strategy.
  • E2 IELTS (YouTube): test walkthroughs and speaking mocks.

Language building#

  • English Collocations in Use: Advanced 💲 · Advanced Grammar in Use 💲 · Academic Word List.
  • Oxford Learner's Dictionaries · Ozdic collocations.

Daily input (Reading & Listening)#

  • Read: The Economist, The Guardian, Scientific American, New Scientist, The Conversation.
  • Listen: BBC Learning English (6 Minute English), BBC In Our Time, TED talks, university lecture podcasts.

Practice partners & feedback#

  • A teacher or a study partner for Writing feedback and Speaking mocks. This matters more than any book.
  • This repo's interactive tools: band calculator, writing timer, speaking practice, flashcards.
