Conversion tables used (approximate)

Conversions vary slightly from test to test, because each version is equated for difficulty. The table follows commonly published official guidance. Overall rounding: average the four bands. A remainder of .25 rounds up to .5, .75 rounds up to the next whole band, and anything below rounds down (e.g. 6.125 → 6.0, 6.25 → 6.5, 6.75 → 7.0).
