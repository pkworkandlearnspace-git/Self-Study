IELTS Academic: Target Band 7.0–7.5#

Roadmap⏱ 2 min readView Markdown source

Goal: an overall band 7.0–7.5 with no skill below 6.5 (a common university requirement; check yours). Time budget: ~8–10 weeks at 5–7 h/week, alongside the olympiads.

💡 Key idea · How each chapter works

Every module is a self-contained chapter: you can learn everything from the page without opening another source. Each section follows the same flow:

📖 definitions → 🧮 formulas / rules → ✍️ worked examples → 🎛️ try it (an interactive tool, right where it helps) → ▶️ videos where they help → 📚 optional books and courses for going deeper.

Every chapter starts with a skip test (skip what you already know) and ends with practice questions (answers hidden), a project to build, common traps, and a done when checklist.

The test at a glance#

Skill Time Format Band 7 needs (approx.)
Listening ~30 min (+ transfer time on paper) 4 parts, 40 questions ~30/40 (7.5 ≈ 32/40)
Reading (Academic) 60 min 3 long texts, 40 questions ~30/40 (7.5 ≈ 33/40)
Writing 60 min Task 1 (≥150 words, data/process/map) + Task 2 (≥250 words, essay). Task 2 counts double. Band 7 on 4 criteria
Speaking 11–14 min Part 1 interview · Part 2 a 2-min talk · Part 3 discussion Band 7 on 4 criteria

Overall band = the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half band (x.25 → x.5, x.75 → next whole band). Raw-score conversions vary slightly between tests, so treat the numbers above as targets. Use the band calculator.

The map#

# Module Time Focus
01 Test strategy & scoring 2 h what examiners reward, the band 7 descriptors
02 Listening 1–2 wk prediction, signpost words, spelling, distractors
03 Reading 2 wk skim/scan, T/F/NG, matching headings, time control
04 Writing Task 1 1–2 wk overview, key features, comparison language
05 Writing Task 2 2–3 wk position, paragraph structure, idea development
06 Speaking 2–3 wk fluency, extending answers, Part 2 structure
07 Vocabulary & grammar for band 7 ongoing collocations, academic words, complex sentences

Writing and Speaking are where most strong students lose marks. Give them ~60% of your time.

Interactive tools → interactive/#

  • Band calculator: raw Listening/Reading scores → bands, and overall band rounding.
  • Writing practice: timed Task 1/Task 2 with a live word count, a prompt bank, and a band-7 self-check.
  • Speaking practice: random Part 1/2/3 questions, a 1-min prep + 2-min talk timer, and recording yourself (if your browser allows the microphone).
  • Flashcards: academic collocations, linking language, Task 1 trend vocabulary.

10-week plan#

Weeks Focus
1 Diagnostic: one full official practice test, under real timing → find your weakest skill. Read module 01.
2–3 Listening + Reading technique (modules 02–03): 1 section a day, review every error.
3–6 Writing (modules 04–05): 2 Task 2 essays + 1 Task 1 per week. Self-check with the descriptors, and get feedback if you can.
4–8 Speaking (module 06): 15 min/day of out-loud practice, recording yourself 2–3×/week.
all Vocabulary (module 07): 10 collocations/day in the flashcards. Read 1 Economist/Guardian/Scientific American article a day.
9–10 3–4 full mock tests under exam conditions. Book the test with a buffer for one retake before your deadlines.
