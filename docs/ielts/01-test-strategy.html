01 · Test Strategy & Scoring#

Module 01⏱ 5 min readView Markdown source

Goal: know exactly what is graded so you practice the right things. · Time: ~2 h

🎯 What you'll learn

By the end of this chapter you can:

  • describe every part of the IELTS Academic test: timing, number of questions, what you produce;
  • convert raw Listening/Reading scores to bands and compute the overall band with the official rounding;
  • name the four criteria for Writing and Speaking and say what band 7 needs in each;
  • choose between paper and computer, and know when One Skill Retake helps;
  • plan your preparation around your weakest skill.

Skip test#

  • You can name the 4 Writing criteria and 4 Speaking criteria, and say what band 7 needs in each.
  • You know how the overall band is rounded.

1 · The test at a glance#

📖 Definition · IELTS Academic format (about 2 h 45 min)
Skill Time Content
Listening ~30 min (+10 min to transfer answers on the paper test) 4 recordings, 40 questions, each heard once
Reading 60 min, no extra transfer time 3 long academic passages, 40 questions
Writing 60 min Task 1: describe a chart/diagram, ≥150 words · Task 2: essay, ≥250 words (worth twice Task 1)
Speaking 11–14 min face-to-face interview in 3 parts; may be on another day up to about a week before or after

Listening, Reading and Writing are taken in one sitting, in that order. Check details with your test centre, as arrangements vary.

📖 Definition · Paper vs computer

The content and scoring are the same. On computer you type your Writing (a word counter helps), see the Listening and Reading questions on screen, and get results faster. Do your mock tests in the format you'll take. One Skill Retake is only available for computer-delivered tests.

2 · How scores work#

🧮 Formula · From raw scores to bands (Listening, Academic Reading)

Each correct answer is 1 mark out of 40. The usual conversion (it can vary slightly between test versions):

Band Listening raw Academic Reading raw
9 39–40 39–40
8.5 37–38 37–38
8 35–36 35–36
7.5 32–34 33–34
7 30–31 30–32
6.5 26–29 27–29
6 23–25 23–26
5.5 18–22 19–22
🧮 Formula · Overall band: average and round

The overall band is the average of the four skill bands, rounded to the nearest half band. Officially: an average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band, and one ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band.

Skill bands Average Overall
7, 7, 6.5, 6.5 6.75 7.0
7, 6.5, 6.5, 6.5 6.625 6.5
7.5, 7, 6.5, 6 6.75 7.0
7, 7, 7, 6 6.75 7.0
6.5, 6.5, 6.5, 6 6.375 6.5
6.5, 6.5, 6, 6 6.25 6.5
🎛️ Try it · Band calculator

Enter your mock-test raw scores and writing/speaking estimates. It applies these tables and the rounding, and tells you what you need in each skill to reach your target.

⚠️ Common mistake · Check the minimum per skill

Many universities require an overall band and a minimum in each skill (e.g. "7.0 overall, no band below 6.5"). A strong Listening score can lift your overall band but cannot rescue a Writing score below the minimum.

3 · What examiners grade in Writing and Speaking#

📖 Definition · Writing: four criteria, 25% each
Criterion Band 7 in plain words
Task Achievement (T1) / Task Response (T2) covers every part of the task. T1: a clear overview of the main trends, differences or stages. T2: a clear position throughout, with main ideas extended and supported
Coherence & Cohesion logical organisation, clear progression, a range of linking devices used appropriately (not mechanically), a clear central topic in each paragraph
Lexical Resource enough range for flexibility and precision, some less common vocabulary, awareness of style and collocation, only occasional errors
Grammatical Range & Accuracy a variety of complex structures, frequent error-free sentences, good control of grammar and punctuation
📖 Definition · Speaking: four criteria, 25% each
  • Fluency & Coherence: speaks at length without noticeable effort; some hesitation is fine; uses a range of connectives and discourse markers.
  • Lexical Resource: uses vocabulary flexibly across topics, some less common and idiomatic words, paraphrases effectively.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: a range of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences.
  • Pronunciation: easy to understand throughout; uses stress, rhythm and intonation to help meaning. Your accent is not a problem; clarity is what counts.
📚 Go deeper (optional)

Read the official public band descriptors for Writing and Speaking (linked from ielts.org and the resources page). Every technique in modules 04–06 follows from them.

4 · General strategy#

💡 Key idea · Accuracy beats ambition

A simple sentence with no errors is better than a complex one that breaks. Band 7 needs frequent error-free sentences, so use complex grammar you control and check your work.

💡 Key idea · Answer the question asked

Off-topic or partly answered tasks cap Task Response, however good the English. Read the prompt twice and plan.

⚠️ Common mistake · Never memorise whole answers

Examiners recognise memorised answers and don't credit them. Memorise structures and collocations, then build fresh answers.

📖 Definition · One Skill Retake

Where available, you can retake one skill (once per original test, within 60 days) instead of the whole test; your new report shows the better result. Only for computer-delivered tests at participating centres, and not every institution accepts it, so check first.

✍️ Worked example · Planning from a diagnostic test

Diagnostic: Listening 31/40 (7.0), Reading 28/40 (6.5), Writing 6.0, Speaking 6.5. Average 6.5. To reach 7.0 overall, the cheapest gains are Writing (6.0 → 6.5 or 7.0 with feedback) and Reading (28 → 30). Plan: 3 essays a week with marking, plus one timed reading passage a day.

Practice (answers hidden)#

  1. Your bands are Listening 7.5, Reading 7, Writing 6.5, Speaking 6.5. Overall?
Answer

Average $27.5/4=6.875$, which rounds to the nearest half band: 7.0.

  1. You score 29/40 in Academic Reading. What band, and how many more correct answers for 7.0?
Answer

6.5; one more (30/40) gives 7.0.

  1. Which Writing task matters more, and how should that shape your time?
Answer

Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1. Spend about 40 minutes on Task 2 and 20 on Task 1; many candidates do Task 2 first so it never gets rushed.

  1. True or false: speaking with a strong accent lowers your Pronunciation band.
Answer

False. The criterion is how easy you are to understand (sounds, stress, rhythm, intonation), not whether you sound like a native speaker.
