Physics Olympiad Resources#

Resources⏱ 1 min readView Markdown source

⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

The core stack#

  1. ⭐ Kevin Zhou's olympiad handouts: complete, free, problem-rich (mechanics, E&M, thermo, waves, relativity, modern). The best single resource.
  2. ⭐ Jaan Kalda's study guides: compact "idea lists" (kinematics, mechanics, E&M, thermodynamics, experimental) with IPhO-level problems.
  3. ⭐ Past IPhO problems with official solutions and marking schemes (below).

Textbooks (💲, pick per module)#

Book Module
Introduction to Classical Mechanics, Morin 02 (and relativity, 06)
Electricity and Magnetism, Purcell & Morin 04
An Introduction to Thermal Physics, Schroeder 03
An Introduction to Error Analysis, Taylor 07
200 Puzzling Physics Problems / 200 More Puzzling…, Gnädig et al. all (problem books)
Physics Olympiad: Basic to Advanced Exercises (Japan PhO committee) all
Problems in General Physics, Irodov all (hard drill)
Fundamentals of Physics, Halliday/Resnick/Walker gap filling

Past problems#

  • ⭐ IPhO official site and the IPhO unofficial archive: past theory and experimental problems, with solutions.
  • APhO (Asian), EuPhO (European), and USAPhO (AAPT Physics Team past exams): excellent intermediate-level sets.
  • Physics Cup (Kalda): very hard, creative problems, with an online competition each year.
  • Your country's national olympiad archive.

Free extras#

  • The Feynman Lectures on Physics: deep intuition.
  • Falstad simulations: circuits, waves, E&M.
  • phyphox: turns your phone into a lab (for module 07).
  • This repo's interactive tools.
