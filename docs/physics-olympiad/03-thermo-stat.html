03 · Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics#

Module 03⏱ 1 min readView Markdown source

Goal: gases, heat engines, entropy, and the Boltzmann factor. · Time: 3–4 weeks

Skip test#

  • Derive the work done in isothermal and adiabatic expansions of an ideal gas.
  • Prove the Carnot efficiency $1 - T_c/T_h$ and why no engine can beat it.
  • Derive $pV = \frac13 Nm\langle v^2\rangle$ from kinetic theory. State equipartition.
  • Use the Boltzmann factor to find the isothermal atmosphere's density profile.
  • Use Stefan–Boltzmann to estimate Earth's equilibrium temperature.

Core ideas#

  • Ideal gas: $pV = nRT$. Internal energy $U = \frac f2 nRT$ ($f$ = degrees of freedom). $C_p - C_V = R$, $\gamma = C_p/C_V$.
  • First law: $dU = \delta Q - p\,dV$. Processes: isobaric, isochoric, isothermal, adiabatic ($pV^\gamma$ = const), polytropic.
  • Second law & entropy: $dS = \delta Q_{rev}/T$. The entropy of an isolated system never decreases. Carnot cycle, refrigerators, COP.
  • Kinetic theory: pressure from collisions, Maxwell speed distribution, mean free path $\lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt2 n\sigma}$, effusion.
  • Boltzmann factor: $P \propto e^{-E/kT}$. Equipartition: $\frac12 kT$ per quadratic degree of freedom.
  • Heat transfer: conduction (Fourier's law, thermal resistance as in circuits), convection (qualitative), radiation (Stefan–Boltzmann $P = \sigma A T^4$, Wien's law $\lambda_{max}T = b$).
  • Phase transitions: latent heat, Clausius–Clapeyron (basic), vapor pressure and humidity.
  • Surface phenomena and real gases: van der Waals (qualitative).

Learn from#

  • ⭐ Kalda: Thermodynamics study guide + Kevin Zhou's thermodynamics (T) handouts.
  • An Introduction to Thermal Physics, Daniel Schroeder 💲: excellent and readable (ch. 1–4, 6).
  • Fundamentals of Physics, Halliday/Resnick/Walker 💲: the basics, if you need them.

Build#

  • Draw p–V diagrams for 5 cycles and compute their efficiencies. Estimate Earth's temperature with and without albedo.
