Linear Fit & Uncertainty

The core skill of the IPhO experimental exam: linearize, fit a straight line, and report the slope and intercept with uncertainties (module 07). Paste your data, pick transforms so theory predicts a line, and read off the result.

Bottom: residuals. A good fit has residuals scattered randomly around 0. A curve in them means the model is wrong.

Formulas used

Unweighted least squares: Δ = NΣX² − (ΣX)², slope m = (NΣXY − ΣXΣY)/Δ, intercept b = (ΣY − mΣX)/N. The scatter s² = Σ(Y − mX − b)²/(N − 2) gives σm = s√(N/Δ) and σb = s√(ΣX²/Δ). If σy is given for every point, a weighted fit is used with w = 1/σY² (σY propagated through the Y transform).
