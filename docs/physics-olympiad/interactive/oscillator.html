Driven Damped Oscillator

ẍ + 2γẋ + ω₀²x = F cos(ωt), with ω₀ = 1. Watch the transient die out and the motion lock onto the drive frequency. Then compare its amplitude with the resonance curve (module 02).

Key results

  • Steady state: A(ω) = F / √((ω₀² − ω²)² + (2γω)²), tan φ = 2γω / (ω₀² − ω²).
  • The peak is at ωres = √(ω₀² − 2γ²) (only if γ < ω₀/√2). The phase lag is exactly π/2 at ω = ω₀.
  • Quality factor Q = ω₀/(2γ) ≈ ω₀/Δω (the FWHM of the energy curve). Large Q = sharp, tall peak and long ring-down.
  • The transient decays as e−γt, so the time to settle is a few times 1/γ.
