The checklist behind the answers

  1. Where does it start? Initial loss ≈ ln C. Much higher → bad init or a scaling bug. Stuck at ln C → the model isn't learning from the labels at all.
  2. Does train loss go down? No → bug or LR far too low. Explodes → LR too high or missing clipping.
  3. Train vs val: gap growing → overfitting. Both high and flat → underfitting.
  4. Shape of the noise: periodic → data order. Rare spikes → numerics or bad batches. Wide band → batch too small or LR too high.
