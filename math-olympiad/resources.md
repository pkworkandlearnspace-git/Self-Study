# Math Olympiad Resources
⭐ = the main resource · 💲 = paid · everything else is free.

## Books by stage
| Stage | Book | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Ramp | ⭐ **The Art and Craft of Problem Solving**, Zeitz 💲 | strategies, first proofs |
| Ramp | AoPS *Volume 1 & 2*, *Intermediate Algebra*, *Intermediate Counting*, *Intro to NT* 💲 | filling gaps |
| National | ⭐ **Problem-Solving Strategies**, Engel 💲 | huge problem bank by technique |
| National | ⭐ **[EGMO](https://web.evanchen.cc/geombook.html)**, Evan Chen 💲 | geometry |
| National | **Modern Olympiad Number Theory**, Khurmi (free PDF, [AoPS thread](https://artofproblemsolving.com/community/c6h2344755)) | number theory |
| National | **[Problem-Solving Methods in Combinatorics](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-0348-0597-1)**, Soberón (Birkhäuser, 2013) 💲 | combinatorics |
| IMO | **Problems from the Book**, Andreescu & Dospinescu 💲 | hard algebra/NT |
| IMO | **Lemmas in Olympiad Geometry**, Andreescu et al. 💲 | geometry lemmas |
| IMO | **Secrets in Inequalities**, Pham Kim Hung 💲 | inequalities |

## Free handouts (the best free material)
- ⭐ **[Evan Chen's handouts](https://web.evanchen.cc/handouts.html)**: inequalities, orders, functional equations, geometry, proof writing.
- ⭐ **[Yufei Zhao's olympiad handouts](https://yufeizhao.com/olympiad/)**: from MOP (the US training camp).
- ⭐ **[OTIS Excerpts (Evan Chen)](https://web.evanchen.cc/textbooks/OTIS-Excerpts.pdf)**: a free book of lecture notes and problems across all four areas, plus advice on writing and practice.
- **[Evan Chen's Napkin](https://web.evanchen.cc/napkin.html)**: higher math for olympiad students (optional, beyond the IMO).

## Problems & community
- ⭐ **[AoPS Contest Collections](https://artofproblemsolving.com/community/c13_contest_collections)**: past olympiads with solutions.
- ⭐ **[IMO official site](https://www.imo-official.org/)**: problems, rules, and results.
- **[AoPS forums](https://artofproblemsolving.com/community)**: ask questions, read solution discussions.
- **[MOHS hardness scale (Evan Chen)](https://web.evanchen.cc/upload/MOHS-hardness.pdf)**: difficulty ratings so you can pick problems at the right level.

## Tools
- **[GeoGebra](https://www.geogebra.org/geometry)**: accurate geometry figures.
- **[This repo's interactive tools](interactive/index.html)**: technique quiz, invariants lab, inequality lab, NT toolkit, modular lab, lattice paths, games lab, triangle centers, power of a point, mock exam timer, flashcards.
