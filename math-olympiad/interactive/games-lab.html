Games Lab

Impartial games are solved by labelling positions P (the player to move loses) and N (the player to move wins). Play Nim against a perfect opponent, then compute P/N tables and Grundy values for any subtraction game.

Click a stone to take it and every stone to its right in that pile. Whoever takes the last stone wins.

P-position (player to move loses)small number = Grundy value
Rule: a position is N iff some move reaches a P-position. Grundy value g(n) = mex{g(n − k)}; P ⟺ g = 0. For a sum of games (play in any one each turn), the sum is P ⟺ the XOR of Grundy values is 0. Nim is the case g(n) = n.
