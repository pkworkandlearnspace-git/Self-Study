Where the residue classes overlap

Combine two congruences at a time: from x ≡ r₁ (mod m₁) write x = r₁ + m₁k and solve m₁k ≡ r₂ − r₁ (mod m₂). It is solvable iff gcd(m₁, m₂) divides r₂ − r₁, and the result is unique mod lcm(m₁, m₂).

Odd p, p | a − b, p ∤ ab: vp(aⁿ − bⁿ) = vp(a − b) + vp(n).
p = 2, a, b odd: if 4 | a − b, the same formula; if n is even, v₂(aⁿ − bⁿ) = v₂(a − b) + v₂(a + b) + v₂(n) − 1.
Use b = −1 for aⁿ + 1 with n odd.
Legendre: vp(n!) = ⌊n/p⌋ + ⌊n/p²⌋ + ⌊n/p³⌋ + … The first term counts multiples of p, the second gives an extra 1 to multiples of p², and so on. Equivalent form: (n − sp(n)) / (p − 1), where sp(n) is the digit sum of n in base p.
