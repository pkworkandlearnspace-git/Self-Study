Invariant & Pigeonhole Lab

Three classic problems you can play with. In each, try to reach the goal first, then watch the quantity in the side panel. When the goal is impossible, that quantity is the proof.

Click two numbers. They are replaced by |a − b|. Keep going until one number is left. Goal: finish with 0.

Drag the points. Try to keep every pair farther apart than the dashed limit.

Split the unit square into k² boxes of side 1/k. With k² + 1 points, some box holds two of them, and two points in one box are at most the box's diagonal √2/k apart.

Click a square, then a neighbouring square, to place a domino. Click a domino to remove it.
